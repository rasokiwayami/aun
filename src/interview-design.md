# Personal context interview — provisional generic baseline

This is the current 200-question prototype bank, not the final OSS question design. Questions tied to one person's historic records have been rewritten for generic use. The redesign brief asks for a fresh, prioritized bank of roughly 300 questions if useful; no user must answer them all.

The purpose is to understand situation-specific values, preferences, constraints, exceptions and changes, not to assign a personality label. Treat answers as source material, model summaries as unconfirmed drafts, user confirmation as scoped and revocable, and execution permission as a separate boundary. Recording an answer does not authorize external actions.

Users may skip, defer, say they do not know, decline, correct earlier answers, or stop. Do not infer beliefs from nonresponse. Ask one useful question at a time. Prioritize questions that change assistance quality and avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive details or third-party identifiers. P0/P1/P2 are provisional usefulness priorities, not human worth or demands to disclose information.

The current runtime stores raw answers before inference, then uses exact source quotes to ground draft state updates. Confirmation, full correction propagation, deletion/export management and everyday context retrieval are future design work. No private imported profile, personal answer or real credential is included in this repository.
